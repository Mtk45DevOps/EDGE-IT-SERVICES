Frequently asked questions

Questions about EDGE?

Does EDGE provide onsite support?

Yes. EDGE offers remote and onsite support in Benoni, Boksburg, Kempton Park and surrounding East Rand areas, subject to availability and agreed travel charges.

Can my business get support without a monthly package?

Yes. Contact EDGE to discuss a once-off support request. The work and price will be agreed before it starts.

Are hardware and software licences included?

No. Hardware, software licences and third-party subscriptions are quoted separately unless a written agreement states otherwise.

Does EDGE provide 24-hour support?

Not currently. Support is provided during agreed business hours.

How do I get started?

Contact EDGE through WhatsApp or email. We will discuss your business size, devices and support requirements.
