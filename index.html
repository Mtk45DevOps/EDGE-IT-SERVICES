FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Does EDGE provide onsite support?

Yes. EDGE offers remote and onsite support in Benoni, Boksburg, Kempton Park and surrounding East Rand areas, subject to availability and agreed travel charges.

Can I request once-off support?

Yes. Contact EDGE to discuss a once-off support request. The work and price will be agreed before it starts.

Are hardware and licences included?

Hardware, software licences and third-party subscriptions are quoted separately unless a written agreement states otherwise.

Does EDGE offer 24/7 support?

Not currently. Support is provided during agreed business hours.
